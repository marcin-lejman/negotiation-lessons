Contract Negotiation

A repeatable method for B2B purchase, service, and distribution negotiations — best price and terms, without burning the relationships that take years to build.

The system so far

PREPARE OPEN CLOSE THE GAP GROW THE PIE DELIVER IT deal sheet → anchor → concession → variable → tactical (0001) (0002) ladder (0003) map (0004) empathy (0005) the sheet bounds the game · the anchor opens it · the ladder spends the budget the map makes spending profitable · the voice keeps the relationship whole

Lessons · ~15 minutes each, quiz included

  1. 0001 The Deal Sheet BATNA → reservation point → aspiration point → ZOPA. One page, three numbers, prepared before every negotiation — and the equivalence trap that makes "cheaper" quotes lie.
  2. 0002 Anchoring: The First Number When to name the first number (more often than procurement instinct says), why precise beats round, and the three-move defense when the anchor is theirs.
  3. 0003 Concession Strategy Never free: labeled, traded, shrinking. Malhotra's four strategies and the pre-planned concession ladder that spends your budget profitably.
  4. 0004 Interests, Not Positions The interests behind a "firm" price, logrolling across valuation gaps, the variable map — and why settling price first destroys your trading currency.
  5. 0005 Calibrated Questions & Tactical Empathy Mirror, label, empathy loop, and the how/what question that hands them your problem. Pressure that doesn't bruise — plus a three-day drill to make it reflexive.
  6. 0006 Multi-Issue Offers (MESOs)planned Presenting several equally-valued packages at once — signaling flexibility while mapping their true priorities.
  7. 0007 Buyer-Side Contract Termsplanned Payment terms, warranties, liability, exit clauses — which commercial terms are worth fighting for, from the buyer's chair.
