# Contract Negotiation Resources

## Knowledge

- [Article: "A Negotiation Preparation Checklist" — Program on Negotiation, Harvard Law School](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/negotiation-preparation-checklist/)
  PON's 32-question pre-negotiation checklist. Use for: structuring preparation before any supplier negotiation; the source for deal-sheet items (BATNA, reservation point, aspiration point, ZOPA, counterpart research).
- [Article: "What is BATNA? Translate Your BATNA to the Current Deal" — PON Harvard](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/batna/translate-your-batna-to-the-current-deal/)
  How to turn an alternative into a usable reservation value, and the trap of comparing non-equivalent options. Use for: anything BATNA/reservation-point related.
- [Topic hub: BATNA & ZOPA — PON Harvard](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/batna-and-zopa/)
  Continuously updated article stream on core deal-zone concepts. Use for: deepening any lesson on deal structure.
- [Book: _Negotiation for Procurement and Supply Chain Professionals_ — Jonathan O'Brien (4th ed., Kogan Page)](https://www.koganpage.com/logistics-supplychain-operations/negotiation-for-procurement-and-supply-chain-professionals-9781398620964)
  The standard procurement-side negotiation text (Red Sheet methodology). Use for: supplier-specific tactics, concession planning, cultural factors, evaluating the sales team across the table. **Closest fit to the mission of any book.**
- [Book: _Getting to Yes_ — Fisher, Ury & Patton (Harvard Negotiation Project)](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-training-daily/negotiation-books-a-negotiation-reading-list/)
  Foundational principled-negotiation text: interests vs. positions, objective criteria, BATNA. Use for: the relationship-preserving side of the mission.
- [Book: _Never Split the Difference_ — Chris Voss](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-training-daily/negotiation-books-a-negotiation-reading-list/)
  Tactical empathy, calibrated questions, labeling. Use for: at-the-table verbal technique once the structural skills are in place.
- [Book: _3D Negotiation_ — Lax & Sebenius (Harvard Business School)](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-training-daily/negotiation-books-a-negotiation-reading-list/)
  Deal design and away-from-the-table moves (setup, parties, sequencing). Use for: later lessons on shaping the deal before the meeting.
- [Article: "To Build Rapport, Be a (Subtle) Copycat" — PON Harvard](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/negotiation-research-you-can-use-to-build-rapport-be-a-subtle-copycat-nb/)
  Independent research support for mirroring/mimicry: highest joint outcomes when both sides mimic; subtlety caveat. Use for: grounding the Voss verbal toolkit (Lesson 0005) in peer-reviewed findings.
- [Glossary: "Empathy Loop" — PON Harvard](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/glossary/empathy-loop/)
  The inquire → respond → verify-understanding cycle. Use for: the listening structure underneath labels and calibrated questions.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/procurement](https://www.reddit.com/r/procurement/)
  Active community of working procurement professionals. Use for: sanity-checking supplier negotiation situations, payment-terms norms, RFQ tactics.
- [r/negotiation](https://www.reddit.com/r/negotiation/)
  General negotiation practice community. Use for: posting anonymized scenarios for critique.
- CIPS (Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply) — [cips.org](https://www.cips.org/)
  Professional body with local branches (including MENA region) and events. Use for: in-person practitioner exchange, formal procurement credibility.
