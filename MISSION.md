# Mission: Contract Negotiation Techniques

## Why
Marcin negotiates B2B purchase contracts on an ongoing, recurring basis — supplier/procurement deals, service & project contracts, and distribution/dealership agreements. He is a practiced negotiator with good instincts but no formal framework. The goal is to replace improvisation with a repeatable method that wins the best price and terms **without burning supplier relationships he'll rely on for years**.

## Success looks like
- Walks into every negotiation with a prepared deal sheet: BATNA, reservation point, aspiration point, and a read on the other side's position
- Sets and defends anchors deliberately instead of reacting to the supplier's first number
- Trades concessions for value (never gives something for nothing) and can name the technique he's using mid-negotiation
- Closes recurring supplier deals at measurably better terms while keeping the relationship warm
- Can review a draft purchase contract and flag the commercial terms worth fighting for (payment terms, warranties, exit clauses)

## Constraints
- Learns alongside a busy job — lessons must be short and immediately applicable to real deals
- Prefers practical technique over academic theory; theory only as needed to support a skill

## Out of scope
- Legal drafting / contract law (knowing *what* to negotiate, not how to write clauses)
- Labor/salary negotiation, M&A deal-making
- Hostage/crisis negotiation beyond borrowed tactical techniques
